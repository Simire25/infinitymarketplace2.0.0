Terms & conditions

  1. Who we are. Infinity MarketPlace is an online shop. By placing an order you agree to these terms.
  2. Prices. All prices are in Nigerian naira (NGN). The price you see at checkout, plus the delivery fee shown there, is the full price. We may correct an obvious pricing error and will tell you before we process your order.
  3. Orders. An order is accepted when we confirm it. We may cancel an order if an item is out of stock, an address cannot be served, or we suspect fraud. If you already paid, we refund you in full.
  4. Payment. You can pay online by card or bank transfer through Paystack, or in cash on delivery where we offer it. We never see or store your card details; Paystack handles them securely.
  5. Your account. Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used it.
  6. Product information. We try to describe and photograph products accurately. Colours on your screen may differ slightly from the real item.
  7. Our responsibility. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under Nigerian consumer law.

Delivery & returns

Delivery

  • We deliver to the address you give at checkout. Please include a landmark and a phone number we can reach.
  • The delivery fee is shown at checkout before you pay.
  • We will call or message you if a rider cannot reach you. If delivery fails twice, the order may be cancelled and refunded, less any delivery cost already incurred.
  • For cash on delivery, please have the exact amount ready. Check your items with the rider before paying.

Problems, returns and refunds

  • If an item arrives damaged, faulty or not as described, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo.
  • We will repair, replace or refund the item. Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery; you pay the return delivery unless the item was faulty or wrong.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method and normally reach you within 5 to 10 working days after we approve them.
  • If you paid for an order that was cancelled, or paid twice by mistake, we refund the extra payment.

Privacy policy

We respect your privacy and handle your personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

  • What we collect: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, order history, and the notes you add to orders. When you pay online, Paystack processes your payment details; we only receive confirmation and the amount.
  • Why: to take and deliver your orders, take payment, contact you about an order, prevent fraud, and keep our accounts as the law requires.
  • Who sees it: our staff who handle orders and delivery, our delivery riders (name, address and phone only for your delivery), Paystack for payments, and our email provider if we send you a receipt. We do not sell your data.
  • How long we keep it: order records for as long as accounting and tax rules require; account details until you ask us to delete the account.
  • Cookies: we use one essential cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser stores your cart and light/dark choice on your own device. We do not use advertising cookies.
  • Your rights: you may ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by contacting us. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
